# Results
Thesis project - Computational Cognitive Neuroscience

## Overview

This research investigates how counterfactual based regret influences causal credit assignment in a multi-armed bandit task. Participants made repeated choices between two of three shapes (circle, square, triangle), receiving delayed feedback about both their chosen and unchosen options. A task-irrelevant random reward was introduced to cause credit misattribution.

Core questions:

Does regret improve credit assignment in addition to reward?
Does a task-irrelevant random reward disrupt credit assignment via misattribution?
